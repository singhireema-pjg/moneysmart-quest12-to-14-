How to Play: Needs vs. Wants

What is a Need?

Needs are basics required for daily living and survival—like shelter, healthy groceries, basic clothing, and medical care. Cover these first!

What is a Want?

Wants are fun extras that make life exciting—designer sneakers, eating out daily, or high-end gadgets. They are optional and can be dialed back when money is tight.

The Balanced Option

A smart compromise between basic survival and high luxury (e.g., home cooking with an occasional pizza night).
